Why Grow Plants?

You don't need an innate green thumb or hours of free time. A single plant can transform how your room feels.

🍃

Cleaner Air

Houseplants naturally filter everyday indoor air impurities while releasing fresh oxygen, making your living room or bedroom feel crisp and revitalizing.

☀️

Better Mood

Surrounding yourself with lush greenery is scientifically proven to lower daily stress, improve mental focus, and add a calming, peaceful presence to your home.

🌿

Easy Hobby

No complicated tools or fussy routines required. Houseplants teach gentle mindfulness with simple periodic check-ins that take less than two minutes a week.

5 Easy Plants to Start With

These five beloved houseplants are tough, forgiving, and virtually unkillable. Pick whichever catches your eye!

🪴

Snake Plant

Sansevieria trifasciata

  • ☀️ Light: Low to bright indirect light
  • 💧 Water: Every 2–3 weeks (soil dry)

Beginner Tip: Thrives on neglect—if you're ever in doubt, wait another week before watering.

🌿

Pothos

Epipremnum aureum

  • ☀️ Light: Low to medium indirect light
  • 💧 Water: Every 1–2 weeks

Beginner Tip: The leafy vines droop slightly when thirsty and perk right back up within hours of a drink.

🎋

ZZ Plant

Zamioculcas zamiifolia

  • ☀️ Light: Low light & fluorescent light
  • 💧 Water: Every 3–4 weeks

Beginner Tip: It stores water in potato-like underground rhizomes, making it virtually drought-proof.

🌾

Spider Plant

Chlorophytum comosum

  • ☀️ Light: Bright indirect light
  • 💧 Water: Once a week (lightly moist)

Beginner Tip: Produces charming baby spider plantlets that you can snip off and pot for friends.

🌵

Aloe Vera

Aloe barbadensis

  • ☀️ Light: Sunny windowsill or direct sun
  • 💧 Water: Every 3 weeks (soak & dry)

Beginner Tip: Keep it by a sunny kitchen window; its soothing leaf gel cools accidental cooking scrapes.
